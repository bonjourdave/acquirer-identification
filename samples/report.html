Appendix: how the list is built

Score = weighted sum of five features, each 0-1, with ratio features shrunk toward the acquirer type's average in proportion to deal count. Sponsors and strategics are ranked separately; the mix sets how many of each are listed. Only buyers with at least one similar deal are listed.

FeatureWeightDefinition
Relevant activity0.40sum over the acquirer's deals of sector weight x size similarity x recency x outcome x ownership/region match, log-scaled to the most active acquirer
Focus0.25average sector weight x size similarity per deal: how much of its activity looks like this target
Size fit0.15similarity of the acquirer's median deal size to the target's
Recency0.10average recency weight of its deals (4-year half-life)
