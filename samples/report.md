## Appendix: how the list is built

**Score** = weighted sum of five features, each 0-1, with ratio features shrunk toward the acquirer type's average in proportion to deal count. Sponsors and strategics are ranked separately, then the mix above sets how many of each are listed.

| Feature | Weight | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant activity | 0.40 | sum over the acquirer's deals of sector weight x size similarity x recency x outcome x ownership/region match, log-scaled to the most active acquirer |
| Focus | 0.25 | average sector weight x size similarity per deal: how much of its activity looks like this target |
| Size fit | 0.15 | similarity of the acquirer's median deal size to the target's |
| Recency | 0.10 | average recency weight of its deals (4-year half-life) |
| Close rate | 0.10 | share of its deals that closed |

**Conviction rubric:** Low = at most 1 similar deal, no deal in the last 3 years, or size fit below 0.5. High = at least 3 similar deals and size fit at least 0.7. Otherwise Medium. Capped at Medium with 2 or fewer similar deals. A *similar deal* is in the target's sector (or a strongly adjacent one, for sponsors) and near its size.
